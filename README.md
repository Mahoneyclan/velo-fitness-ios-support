# Velo Fitness support

## About the app

Velo Fitness is an iPhone and iPad fitness dashboard that runs entirely on your device.

- **Cycling:** your ride history from Strava and Garmin Connect (beta), with charts of distance, time, elevation, heart rate, power and more.
- **Boxing:** punch metrics from Garmin boxing workouts recorded with the f3b Boxing app.
- No account with us and no server: your data stays on your device.

## Get help

Questions, problems or ideas? Email **[mahoneyclanapps+velofitness@gmail.com](mailto:mahoneyclanapps+velofitness@gmail.com)**.

To help us answer quickly, please include:

- what you were doing and what happened
- your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version
- the app version (shown in the app's Settings or About screen, or in the App Store)
- a screenshot, if it helps

We read every message, and usually reply within a few days.
